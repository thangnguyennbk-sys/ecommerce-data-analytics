# E-commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and delivery operations.

## Business Objective

The objective is to identify key revenue drivers, valuable customer segments, product performance trends, and operational factors that may affect customer satisfaction.
